About & privacy

Face Check gives a light-hearted, opinion-based estimate from one photo. Scores are approximate and depend heavily on lighting, angle and camera. They aren't a measure of your worth, and they aren't medical advice.

Your data: the photo is processed in your browser and is never sent to any server. Nothing is stored, and there are no accounts, analytics or ads. The face-detection model downloads once from the jsDelivr CDN.

Intended for adults (18+). Product links are examples, not sponsored.
